FAQ

Common questions

How long does a session take?

Sessions take 45–60 minutes, plus a short health screening before we start. A nurse usually arrives within 20–60 minutes of confirming.

Is it safe?

Every session is administered by a licensed nurse using sealed, sterile, single-use supplies, and starts with a brief health screening. If something isn’t right for you, the nurse will tell you and won’t proceed. IV therapy is not a substitute for emergency care — for emergencies, call 911.

Can you come to a yacht?

Yes. We visit boats docked at marinas in Cabo San Lucas and San José del Cabo. Send us the marina and slip number on WhatsApp when you message.

How do I pay?

Payment is by card or cash after the session. Pricing is confirmed in USD on WhatsApp before a nurse is dispatched, and there is no booking fee.
